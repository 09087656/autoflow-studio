## How to Choose the Right Automation Tool

**Ask yourself:**

1. **What's your budget?**
   - Under $50/mo → Make or n8n
   - $50-200/mo → Zapier or ActiveCampaign
   - $200+ → HubSpot or enterprise tools

2. **What's your technical skill?**
   - Beginner → Zapier or HubSpot
   - Intermediate → Make

3. **What do you want to automate?**

## Mistakes to Avoid

**1. Automating broken processes**
- Fix the process first, then automate

**2. Over-complicating workflows**
- Start simple, add complexity later

**3. Not testing thoroughly**
- Test every workflow 10+ times before going live

**4. Ignoring errors**
- Set up error notifications
- Monitor your automations

**5. Not documenting**
- Write down what each automation does
- Future you will thank you
