- Early-stage startups (Seed to Series A)
- SaaS companies
- Agencies (marketing, consulting)
- Solo founders with < 10 employees

**Pain points to mention:**
- Lead generation
- Email outreach
- Social media management
- Data entry
- Reporting
- CRM management

**Industries:**
- SaaS
- Marketing agencies
- Consulting

## Outreach Plan

**Day 1:** 20 cold DMs (Template A/B mix)  
**Day 2:** 20 cold DMs (Template C/D mix)  
**Day 3:** Follow-up round 1  
**Day 5:** 20 new cold DMs  
**Day 7:** Follow-up round 2  
**Day 10:** 20 new cold DMs

**Target:** 100 DMs/week → 5-10 responses → 1-2 clients

---

## Response Handling

**If positive response:**
→ "Great! What's the biggest time-sink for your team right now?"  
→ Qualify: budget, timeline, decision-maker  
→ Book 15min call (Calendly link)

**If "not now":**
→ "No worries! Mind if I check back in a month?"  
→ Add to follow-up list

**If "tell me more":**
→ Share portfolio link (Notion page)  
→ "We recently did X for Y. Similar to what you need?"

---

## Success Metrics

- **DMs sent:** 100/week
- **Response rate target:** 5-10%
- **Call booking rate:** 20-30% of responses
- **Conversion rate:** 25-50% of calls
- **Target clients:** 2-4/week
